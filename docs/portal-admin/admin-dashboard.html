Admin Dashboard

The admin dashboard provides an organization wide view of the data collected by sayhii. This includes information that can help understand user participation and rollout of the application as well as analyzing responses by theme.

Organization Overview​

The Organization Overview tab displays high level information about participation along with a list of users that are in sayhii along with their registration status. Users with a status of Confirmed have successfully configured themselves to answer questions on at least one device. Other metrics displayed on this page include:

  • Expected Users - The total number of users that have been added to sayhii through a file upload or HRIS integration
  • Confirmed Users - The number of users that have successfully configured at least one device for answering questions
  • Participation Rate - Percentage of Confirmed Users relative to Expected Users
  • Questions Answered - The total number of responses to questions across all users in the organization
  • Questions Skipped - The total number of questions that have been skipped (using the Skip link in the app) by all users in the organization

Organization Timeline​

It can be valuable to understand how users are participating with sayhii over time. The Registration Progress graph shows the growth of the overall sayhii user base within your organization. In most cases, we see a steady increase right after system rollout and then fairly steady participation after the initial increase unless the organization experience significant restructuring.

The Question Participation graph provides insight into the pattern of users answering questions. In most organizations, we see a distinct weekly pattern with most questions answered on weekdays and far fewer questions answered on the weekend. It is also useful to track skipped questions to understand if there are populations that would just rather not participate.

Theme Analysis​

The theme analysis page allows administrators to look at individual theme scores over time and by department.

Department Comparison​

Understanding where problems exist within an organization can be extremely helpful in determining who needs to be involved in conversations. The matrix at the top of this dashboard provides a comparision of the average responses over the past three months across all departments. This view helps to understand if there are specific themes where the whole organization may be struggling equally, or perhaps specific teams that need to focus more on specific themes. In addition to the departmental comparison, you may also gain insight on this page from the average answers across the whole organization for specific questions. The table at the bottom right displays the lowest scoring questions by average answer by theme.
