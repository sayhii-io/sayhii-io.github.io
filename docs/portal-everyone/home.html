Home

The sayhii homepage is a focus on you. We provide a dashboard to help you understand yourself, your organization, and the way your peers are answering questions related to specific themes. This dashboard provides three focus areas:

  1. Vitals - These measure different aspects of your personal view on work and your ability to stay motivated.
  2. Organizational Scorecard - Most of the sayhii daily questions focus on the organization's perceptions of different themes that impact the overall cohesiveness of the organization. This scorecard provides a top-level view of how the organization is doing.
  3. Themes - After looking at the scorecard, you can dive a bit deeper in to the scores for each theme.

Vitals​

The vitals that sayhii measures through daily questions are:

  • Resources - This score reflects your perception of the resources available to you. These resources might be specific job related resources, or the availability of your organization to provide support when you need it.
  • Demands - What are the physical, emotional, and psychological demands of your job? We help you reflect on the strength of those job demands and consider how that might be impacting you.
  • Work Life Balance - Are you able to do things other than just work? This score reflects how you respond to questions about your availability outside the workplace. Does this impact your focus at work?

Organizational Scorecard​

The overall scorecard provides insight into the average responses to questions across the whole organization over the prior 3 month period.

  • Organizational Wellness - A top level score of the motivation of the organization. The higher the percentage, the more cohesive and motivated the team.
  • Overall Engagement - Reflects how everyone feels about the organization as a whole. The higher the percentage, the better the overall internal perception of the organization.
  • Vitals - These reflect the same types of scores as the individual vitals, but provides an average view across everyone in the organization.
  • Theme Scores - Like other percentages, the higher, the better. These scores provide a window into how the organization perceives the organization in these different themes:
    • Autonomy - Do people in the organization feel that they have the right amount of freedom to get things done effectively?
    • Change - How well does the organization deal with change?
    • Communication - Does the organization communicate well?
    • Diversity/Inclusion - Are people able to be themselves and contribute based on their personal experiences?
    • Environment - Does the organization attempt to provide an envrionment that supports employees and the work that needs to be done?
    • Equity/Equality - Are expectations and responsibilities similar for employees in the organization?
    • Innovation - Does the organization allow individuals to improve the way desired outcomes are achieved?
    • Performance - Do teams, managers, and the organization meet performance expectations?
    • Recognition - How well does the organization celebrate success?
    • Support - The organization's ability to provide what is necessary to achieve stated goals.
    • Talent Management & Development - Does the organization value employee growth and provide opportunities to learn new things?
    • Trust - How well do people in the organization do what they say they are going to do?

Themes​

The themes page of the dashboard provides additional details on the theme scores.

  • The timeline provides a comparison of your own scores for the selected theme with the whole organization.
  • The matrix on the right compares the organization's scores for each theme with your department
  • The list of questions show the questions for the selected theme that have the lowest average answers across all user's answers in the organization
  • The sub-theme radar chart at the bottom right breaks down the theme further to show the particular areas of interest. The areas that are closer to the center of the chart need more focus within the organization
