Profile

Have you ever wondered if you can "change your answers" from your original registration in sayhii? The answer is absolutely YES!

The profile page allows you to update information about your department, job function, hire date, and other information about yourself.

You can also enable the ability for sayhii to ask questions in the portal.

sayhii Questions​

You can choose to answer questions in the portal, just like you would in the desktop app. If you turn this feature on, you'll receive your sayhii questions when you log in to the portal. You'll only get your questions once on any given day, no matter what platform you're using. If you also have the desktop app installed, you'll only see questions in the portal if you haven't already answered them in the desktop app.

To turn on questions in the portal, you'll want to go to the bottom of your profile and make sure the "Answer sayhii Questions in Portal" checkbox is checked. If you change the state of the checkbox, make sure you hit the SAVE button to save that change.
