sayhii Data Privacy, Retention, and Destruction Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2026
Owner: Chief Technology Officer
Applies to: sayhii production systems and customer data processed to deliver the sayhii Services

1. Purpose​

sayhii is committed to protecting the privacy and data of our customers and their employees. This policy describes how sayhii collects, uses, retains, anonymizes, and destroys personal and survey data as part of the sayhii Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) employee engagement platform.

This policy serves as the authoritative internal reference for data handling practices at sayhii and is intended to align with customer contracts, privacy notices, and applicable legal and regulatory expectations.

2. Scope​

This policy applies to customer data processed in connection with the sayhii Services, including data stored in:

  • AWS DynamoDB
  • AWS S3
  • Snowflake
  • Auth0

Certain supporting systems (such as logs, backups, monitoring, and analytics tools) are not considered authoritative sources of customer data and are governed by separate retention and lifecycle controls.

3. Data We Collect​

sayhii processes three primary categories of data:

3.1 Identity and Demographic Data​

Information used to identify users and support organizational reporting, including but not limited to:

  • Name and work email address
  • Department and manager relationships
  • Job title, job function, and hire date
  • Demographic attributes (e.g., age, gender, race, ethnicity), when provided
  • Machine/device id

3.2 Survey Response Data​

Responses submitted by employees to sayhii questions, including:

  • Likert-scale responses
  • Any free-text or open-response fields (when applicable)

3.3 Usage and Technical Data​

Information about how users interact with the sayhii platform, such as:

  • Application usage patterns
  • Page views and errors
  • Diagnostic and performance data

4. How We Use Data​

sayhii uses collected data for the following purposes:

  • Service delivery: Delivering survey questions and collecting responses
  • Reporting: Providing anonymized, aggregated insights to customer organizations
  • Customization: Supporting monthly activations, group-based insights, and internal analytics
  • Benchmarking: Generating anonymized benchmarks across organizations
  • Product improvement: Improving platform functionality, performance, and usability

Personal data is never used for purposes unrelated to service delivery or product improvement.

5. Aggregation and Anonymization​

sayhii is designed to protect employee anonymity.

  • Individual survey responses are never shared with an organization in a way that identifies the respondent.
  • Reporting is limited to aggregated groups that meet defined minimum thresholds (e.g., groups of five or more).
  • When data is used for benchmarking or cross-organizational analysis, it is anonymized and aggregated prior to use.

Anonymization includes the removal of direct identifiers.

6. Data Retention During Active Participation​

While a customer organization is actively using sayhii:

  • Identity, demographic, survey, and usage data are retained as needed to provide the Services.
  • Data retention is limited to what is reasonably necessary for service delivery, reporting, support, and security.
  • Access to sensitive data is restricted based on role and business need.

7. Organization Termination and Data Destruction​

7.1 Destruction Triggers​

Organization-level data destruction is initiated under one of the following conditions:

  • Standard termination: Sixty (60) days after contract termination, unless otherwise requested.
  • Customer-initiated deletion: Upon an authorized request from the customer organization, without the standard delay.

Individual employees do not directly initiate organization-level deletion requests.

7.2 Data Destroyed (Hard Delete)​

As part of full organization deletion, sayhii performs hard deletion of:

  • All personally identifiable information (PII)
  • Organization-identifying data and tenant mappings
  • Demographic attributes and manager relationships
  • Free-text survey responses and unstructured content
  • Any datasets that could enable re-identification of anonymized data

Deletion is performed in authoritative systems, including DynamoDB, S3, Snowflake, and Auth0.

7.3 Data Retained (Anonymized)​

After deletion, sayhii may retain:

  • Likert-scale survey responses stripped of direct identifiers
  • Non-identifying usage and performance data

Retained data is moved to a segregated anonymized data system and may be used for:

  • Product development and improvement
  • Statistical analysis
  • Future benchmarking using aggregated outputs only

sayhii does not attempt to re-identify individuals or organizations from anonymized retained data.

8. Systems and Deletion Methods​

  • Destruction of PII and organization-identifying data is performed as a hard delete.
  • Destruction processes are designed to be idempotent and may be safely re-run to ensure completeness.
  • Deletion activities are logged and monitored.

9. Systems Out of Scope for Authoritative Deletion​

The following systems are not considered authoritative sources of customer data:

  • Application and security logs
  • Backups and backup snapshots (immutable)
  • Monitoring and diagnostic systems
  • Third-party analytics tools

Residual data in these systems is retained only for limited periods based on operational and security needs. Backups are subject to lifecycle rotation, and PII is removed through standard expiration and deletion processes.

10. Verification and Oversight​

  • Data destruction events are logged with timestamps, scope, and outcomes.
  • Destruction processes may be re-run to remediate partial failures.
  • Periodic reviews are conducted to confirm that destruction aligns with this policy.

Evidence may include logs, reports, or review records.

11. Roles and Responsibilities​

  • Engineering / Operations: Implement and maintain data handling and destruction processes
  • Customer Success: Validate and route customer-initiated deletion requests
  • Security / Privacy Owner: Maintain this policy and oversee compliance

12. Policy Review and Updates​

This policy is reviewed at least annually and updated as needed to reflect changes in systems, services, or legal and contractual requirements.
